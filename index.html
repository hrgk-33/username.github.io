02 // ABOUT ME

Curiosity-Driven Builder & Grade 12 Student

I am a Grade 12 student in Kerala, India, focused on building practical technology, educational tools, and independent cinema. For me, learning goes far beyond textbooks and exam halls — it means questioning how everyday systems work, designing real prototypes, and sharing ideas through books and film.

My projects span hardware and product design with the distraction-free ToL ID Phone & Artha AI, international EdTech with Eduhex UK — Focus Lab (showcased at BeTT 2026 in London), authoring three books on student agency in the age of AI (Author of Three), and directing the AICFF-selected film Mahaakaavya: The NEET Story.
